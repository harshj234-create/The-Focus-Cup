Why The Focus Cup

Studying Shouldn’t Feel This Hard.

If any of these sound like your day, you are not alone.

Noise and distractions at home

TV, guests, traffic, and family chats make it hard to stay in one chapter for long.

No proper desk or chair

Studying on a bed or floor for hours leads to back pain and low energy.

Power cuts and weak Wi-Fi

A cut in the middle of a mock test or a live lecture breaks your whole plan.

No routine, low motivation

At home every hour feels the same. It is easy to delay and hard to restart.

No quiet spot for online classes

Hard to unmute, ask a doubt, or hear the teacher clearly with noise around.

Nowhere for group study or calls

Libraries ask you to stay silent. Cafés are loud and want you to keep ordering.

You deserve a space that helps you show up, focus, and make progress every day.

    FAQ

    Questions you might have.

    Is The Focus Cup already open?

    Not yet. The Focus Cup is in the research and planning stage. We are using this survey to decide whether to open it and what it should include.

    Where will The Focus Cup be located?

    The exact location is not fixed. We will choose an area in Meerut based on where survey responses and early-access sign-ups come from.

    How much will it cost?

    Prices are not decided. We plan to offer daily, weekly and monthly options, and the price question in the survey will help us keep it affordable for students.

    Will there be Wi-Fi and charging points?

    Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi, charging sockets and power backup are part of the core plan.

    Will there be food and drinks?

    Drinking water is part of the core plan. Tea, coffee, cold drinks and snacks are planned based on demand, so tell us in the survey if you want them.

    Can I study with friends?

    The main zone will be silent, but we plan a separate group study pod for small groups if enough people ask for it.

    Can I take online classes or calls?

    A private call booth is planned for online classes, interviews and calls. Whether we build one, or more than one, depends on demand.

    How can I get launch updates?

    Join the early-access list above, or tick “I would like updates” at the end of the survey. We will message you only when there is real news.

    Is filling out the survey free?

    Yes, completely free. It takes about 5 minutes and does not sign you up for anything.
